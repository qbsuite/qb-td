Pool grids on the public stats page

The New layout lists each pool’s standings, but not who has played whom. Below are three ways to add a square grid for each pool. The page is ACF Fall @ UIUC with made-up results: rounds 1 to 4 are in and rounds 5 to 7 are still to come, so the grids show a pool partway through.

A. A grid beside each table

Every pool keeps its table and gains a grid of scores to its right, in the same order as the table. On a phone the grid sits under the table.

Teams are numbered by their place, so a reader matches a column to a team by its number.

B. Results inside the table

The table itself gains one narrow column per opponent with a W, an L, or the round the game is in. It is the most compact, and adds nothing new to the page.

It shows no scores, and on a phone the extra columns are reached by scrolling the table sideways.

C. A Table or Grid switch

Each pool has a switch. The grid replaces the table and has team names along both sides, scores in every square and the record at the end.

The table and the grid are never on screen together. The first pool is shown as Grid and the others as Table.
