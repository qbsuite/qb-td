Tossup box score

A grid at the top of each game on the Scoreboard (New layout). It has one row per team and one column per tossup read. A cell shows what that team got on the tossup (15, 10 or −5) and how it did on the bonus. Hovering a cell shows who buzzed. The player and bonus tables stay below it.
