Zoom and fit in the full screen schedule editor

In full screen today the grid stays at its normal size, so with 10 rooms the last room is cut off, long team names are shortened and the playoff rounds are below the fold. Below are three ways to use the screen better. Each artboard is the real editor for the local 22-team copy of ACF Fall @ UIUC on a 1440 by 813 laptop screen. A green outline marks what each proposal adds.

A. Zoom buttons

Minus and plus buttons beside the exit symbol make the grid smaller or larger in steps, and the number between them resets it to 100%. The TD decides how much to see.

Nothing happens until the TD presses a button, and the text gets smaller as more fits.

B. Fit to the screen

Full screen opens with the grid already sized so that every room fits across, and it re-fits when the window changes or a room is added. Minus and plus still work, and Fit goes back to automatic.

On a big schedule the fitted text can get small, so it would stop shrinking at 60% and scroll from there.

C. Fill the width without shrinking

The selection panel is tucked away until something is clicked, and the room columns stretch to share the whole width. Text stays full size and long team names have room.

It does not help with height, and past about 11 rooms on this screen the grid would scroll sideways again.
