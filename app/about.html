About

qb-td runs the logistics of a quizbowl tournament from one link. The tournament director sets up rooms, packets, a roster and a schedule; each moderator gets a room link that hands them the right packet and takes their finished game; and everyone else follows the schedule and the stats on a public page as games come in.

There are no accounts. Creating a tournament gives you an admin link, and that link is the only key to it. You have a week to set up; pressing Start tournament opens the rooms, and every link then works for 48 hours. Stats export to YellowFruit and to a standard HTML stat report.

qb-td is part of qbsuite.
