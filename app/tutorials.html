    Example Tournament

    Below is an example tournament on a simulated tournament.

    Tournament directorThe TD can set up the tournament, set the round number, watch games arrive, rule on protests, and export stats. ModeratorModerators are shown their room's schedule and are provided links to MODAQ pages with teams and packet loaded in. Player or spectatorPlayers and the public can view schedules, stats, category stats, and buzzpoints if the TD chooses to enable them.
