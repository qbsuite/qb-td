Release Notes for 9/27/2026

[ LIVE HUB ]
- Added "Open the next round automatically": opens the next round once every room with a game in the current round has started.
- Room chips show which rooms have started the current round.
- "Advance to Round N" is always shown, and says when the next round has no packet yet.
- Auto-advance says when it's waiting on the next round's packet.
- The Live Hub refreshes on its own every 30 seconds and when you come back to the tab.
- "Games in" now reads "games uploaded".

[ SETUP ]
- Tournaments now get 7 days of setup. Press Start tournament on the day; every link closes 48 hours after Start.
- Room links show "Tournament hasn't started" until Start.
- The setup checklist now covers MODAQ Settings and Stats settings.
- Moved the buzzpoints switch and password to Stats settings.
- Removed room reopening. Rooms now close with the tournament.
- New tournaments start with the public page on.
- Stats settings is checked once you've opened it.
- Packets + Tiebreakers now says what to upload and how packets reach the rooms.

[ READER ]
- The scheduled matchup is locked in. Switching teams asks for confirmation.
- Moderators pick starters on the start card, any number per team.

[ CATEGORIES ]
- Added a Questions view: tossup conversion, power, neg and average buzz; bonus PPB and Easy/Medium/Hard conversion.
- Bonus difficulty comes from the packet's e/m/h marks, or is ranked by conversion rate when there are none.
- Added BB (bouncebacks): tossups won after the other team missed. Does not change points.
- Fixed category counts showing readings instead of questions (doubled with two rooms).

[ BUZZPOINTS ]
- Added By Category.

[ UI ]
- New filter chips on the public and set pages.
- Labels are now in Title Case.

[ HUB ]
- Tournaments can be removed from "Tournaments on this device". The tournament itself is kept.

[ MISC ]
- Added this changelog, linked from the tournament hub.
