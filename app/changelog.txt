- Uploads is a rooms by rounds grid. Click a cell to see its files, move one to another room, or delete it.

- Live now shows a heading per bracket with how many games are in and which brackets are still a round behind.
- Flags a room that uploaded a game for a round it hasn't reached, or started a round without its last game in.
- Advance all, or advance a single bracket from the Auto-advance list. Set round puts every bracket on that round.
- Uploads groups rooms by bracket, with Prelims and Playoffs marked over the rounds.

- One step list replaces the checklist and tabs. Order: Rooms, Roster, Schedule, Packets, MODAQ Settings, Public page.
- Packets shows one row per round with the packet assigned to it, and Upload or Replace per round.
- The number of rounds comes from the schedule; Add a round for more.
- A roster file from another tool is saved in a form MODAQ can read.
- Fixed a just-generated schedule sometimes disappearing after a roster change.
- Fixed Save format sometimes saving the previous preset.
- "Bucket" links are now "Upload page".
- Add &debug to the hub's URL once to load test rosters from the Roster step; it stays on in that browser until &debug=0.
- Renaming a room now updates the schedule and the public page too.

[ SCHEDULE ]
- New schedule editor: rooms grouped by bracket (Pool A, Championship, 1st place...), round numbers and rooms stay in view while scrolling.
- Drag a team onto another to swap, onto Byes to bench it; drag a game onto another game or an empty room.
- Click a team or a game for the same moves in a side panel, plus Undo, Redo and Discard.
- Checks list teams booked twice, teams with no game, half-empty games and repeat pairings.
- By team and Brackets views; filter the grid to one bracket; Compact rows for big fields.
- Generate options: random seeding, shuffled round order, rooms kept, blocked, shuffled or rotated, with a reusable seed.

[ READER ]
- New start screen: round buttons, the scheduled matchup, starter checklists, this room's schedule.
- With pools or playoff brackets: the header names the room's bracket, and its schedule is split by bracket.

- New layout. Drop the .qbj and the MODAQ game file together.

[ PUBLIC PAGE ]
- Schedule has Now (live round and up next) and All rounds, with pool filters.
- Winners are highlighted in green.
